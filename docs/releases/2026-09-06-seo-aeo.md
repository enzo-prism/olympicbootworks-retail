## Search guidance behind the work

Google recommends normal crawl/index fundamentals, original useful expertise and accurate business data for AI visibility; it does not require special AI files or mass-produced question variants: [AI optimization guide](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/ai-optimization-guide).

Canonical signals should agree across links, sitemap and metadata: [canonical guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/consolidate-duplicate-urls). Structured data must represent the business and visible content accurately: [LocalBusiness](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/local-business), [Product snippets](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/product-snippet).

Google retired FAQ rich results in May 2026: [Search documentation updates](https://developers.google.com/search/updates). FAQ markup here only describes the same visible answers; it carries no rich-result promise. No self-serving business review rating was added.

The current wildcard robots rule permits search crawlers, including OAI-SearchBot. Model training controls are separate from search discovery: [OpenAI crawler documentation](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/bots). No training-policy changes were made.
